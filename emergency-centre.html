EMERGENCY CENTRE

One connected emergency view.

The FYNDER Emergency Centre is presented as a centralized view for authorized response personnel to manage active safety incidents.

It connects the platform's reporting, location, alert, communication and emergency-service tools so incidents can be assessed and routed to appropriate response channels.
