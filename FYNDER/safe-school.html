NIGERIA'S SAFE SCHOOL CONTEXT

A technology model for secure learning environments.

The supplied FYNDER document describes Safe School as one of FYNDER's three integrated safety models. It connects families, students, schools, authorized staff and emergency-support channels.

The document also records the historical context of Nigeria's Safe School Initiative: it was launched in May 2014, with 7 May 2014 recorded as the establishment date during the World Economic Forum on Africa in Abuja. FYNDER should present this as context and not as a claim that FYNDER itself is the government programme.

SAFE SCHOOL MEASURES

A layered approach to school safety

01

Physical security

Fencing, secure gates, security personnel, CCTV and other security equipment.

02

Early warning

Identify threats such as kidnapping, violence, communal conflict and disasters before escalation.

03

Emergency preparedness

Response plans, evacuation procedures and drills.

04

Safeguarding & reporting

Mechanisms for reporting abuse, violence and other threats and referring cases appropriately.

05

Communication

Channels connecting schools, communities and safety/response agencies.

06

Emergency support

Connected access to appropriate emergency-support facilities.
