SAFE CORRIDOR

Safer journeys from departure to destination.

Safe Corridor is FYNDER's travel-safety network connecting passengers, drivers, transport companies, emergency contacts and authorized responders.

The model is designed around current location and route, journey progress, road status and safety alerts, a verified driver network and journey sharing.
